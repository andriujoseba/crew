### Added

- The builder engine counts a PR's review rounds and names the round cap to the
  session when the fifth round closes without full approval (#502).

### Changed

- The engine holds its panel request on a PR at the round cap, so the successor
  is the only PR the panel is asked for (#502).
- A fifth round that closes with the panel's unanimous approval converges and
  hands off as usual, and is never named for the cut (#502).

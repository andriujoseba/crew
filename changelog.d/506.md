### Added

- A release candidate carries its own drill record, `drills/X.Y.Z-rcN.md`, and
  `drills/README.md` states the ladder the release doors already implement
  (#506).
- A release guard refuses a final tree whose diff over its last candidate's tag
  touches anything but `VERSION`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `changelog.d/` and `drills/`
  (#506).
- The rc-ladder guard reads the candidate tags as well as the shipped tree's
  records, so deleting a record cannot lower the anchor it is measured against
  (#506).
- The rc-ladder guard refuses a published candidate whose record is absent from
  the shipped tree, and a candidate tag it cannot resolve (#506).

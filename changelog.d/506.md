### Added

- A release candidate carries its own drill record, and `drills/README.md`
  states the ladder: one record per version, so a long window drills a
  candidate once and the final's evidence anchors to that published tag
  (#506).
- A release guard refuses a final tree whose diff over its last candidate's
  tag touches anything but `VERSION`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `changelog.d/` and
  `drills/`: the tree that ships must be the tree that was drilled (#506).
- The rc-ladder guard refuses a candidate tag it cannot resolve rather than
  passing — an unreachable anchor otherwise reads exactly like a window that
  cut no candidate at all (#506).

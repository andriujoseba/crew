### Added

- `kimi` sessions record their token usage on `SESSION END`, read from the session's own `wire.jsonl` artifact after the CLI exits, since kimi reports no usage on stdout (#571).
- A kimi session's figures are the sum across its steps rather than any single record, `token_usage` being per step on that artifact (#571).
- `input_other` is recorded as the contract's uncached `input_tokens` and the two cache counters as themselves; no cost is reported, because kimi publishes none (#571).
- A kimi session's id is pinned by the engine rather than minted by the CLI, which is what makes its usage artifact addressable at all (#571).
- A kimi session whose id could not be pinned, or whose artifact is missing or unreadable, records its usage as absent rather than approximating it from the filesystem (#571).

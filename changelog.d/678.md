  nothing else: the weekly `crew reset --all` is deferred to `0.1.4`, because
  the restore it performs takes `duty.log` with it and clears the resume

  reinstated by uncommenting; a comment block carries the deferral and names
  what returns it (#678).

  its version interlock, its refusals, the host lock and the job log all ship
  exactly as they were, and only the schedule moved (#678).
- `shared/docs/host-maintenance.md` states what is scheduled, what is not and
  why, keeps the price/period reasoning the reversal is argued from, and names
  the disk consequence the deferral leaves with #606 (#678).

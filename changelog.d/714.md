### Fixed

- The rehearsal's phase 2 no longer refuses an identity that carries an
  `attention` demand in another repository. It records each one as a census row
  and runs, so the operator's own account is a supported drill identity and a
  round no longer needs a throwaway one (#714).

### Added

- The rehearsal now asserts the registry bound on the phase-2 attention tick,
  per parked demand: the engine's suppressed report names it, no attention
  session is launched outside the sandbox, and no demand draws a new pickup
  comment. Any miss is a `FAIL` row and stops the round (#714).

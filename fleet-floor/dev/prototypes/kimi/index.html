how should kimi be represented — two live prototypes

Written in the fleet-floor grammar — canvas 2D, plate()/rim/emissive buffers, no assets, no deps — the same way claude's unit is drawn in fleet-floor/src/app.js, so either one ports into buildKimi() directly. Both are live below: use the state buttons under each unit.
proto E · JUGGERNAUT (KM-09X)
The last word. A siege torso on a torch — no legs, no props, three huge shapes and nothing spindly anywhere: a monolithic chest with a caged reactor furnace dead centre, pauldrons the size of engine cowls with grav bells glowing under them, a head buried under a scowling chevron brow (one pink optic, one amber — the glass eye ships), and two hammer gauntlets held in an A-stance. The waist is scale-armour leaves over a vector-vane nozzle cowl; the jet underlights the whole machine. Offline it settles onto its own knuckles — fists on the deck, head down. Even dead it rests like a fighter.
proto A · KM-04 “Tender”
The evolution. Same hovering screen-face drone — but assembled the way claude's unit is: two shells with a parting gasket, a visible gimbal window with trunnion bearings and a braided cable loop, a crown intake feeding a ducted repulsor whose rotor visibly stops offline, jointed ears, a bolted sensor mast, and a service history (replacement panel tacked over a scorch, grime at the bolts). The face the fleet already loves stays exactly as it is.
proto B · SELENE
The rethink. If I choose for myself: I am Moonshot's machine, so the unit is an orrery — a faceted pearl core bolted at an equatorial flange, two gimbal rings with light running in their channels, three emitter prongs focusing a gravity lens. The face is a moon-phase display and the phase is the state: gibbous when working, crescent when idle, new moon with a signal-collapse line when offline — and the rings lose lock and rest on the core.
proto D2 · DARKSIDE-II
The one that survived — three times. Portrait, a ton of armour, and it flies the way a nuclear torch flies: no props, a caged reactor in a massive V-keel driving a column of fire into the deck. Attitude jets for steering, cables and coolant lines on the outside, hazard chevrons, a trefoil stencil. Use the battle buttons: each pass is a fight it took damage in and walked away from heavier — a salvaged sponson, a splinted keel, an amber glass eye. Offline, the torch dies and a ton of metal sets down on outriggers.
proto C · YUE, the Archivist
Entirely new — the one I actually want. The drone idea kept, the flying-TV fixed by giving the body a job: the torso is a rack of memory cartridges, and the unit perpetually reads an endless ribbon of glyph-light that orbits it and feeds a reader slot in the chest — long context made physical. Two dots in a visor slot, but seated in a helm. It stands on a column of moonlight. Offline, the ribbon goes dark and hangs slack, and the head bows.

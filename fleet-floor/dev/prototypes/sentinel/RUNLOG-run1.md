## Read on the run

Every loop's cited defects were verified fixed at the cited coordinates
(pixel-sampled before/after), and the score never moved. The verdicts kept
rotating a different "nothing is grounded / nothing emits" #1 while sampling
different pixels each time — several claims did not reproduce at region scale
(e.g. S5's "haze sheet 18x brighter" is the strobe tube's shaft, unchanged
between renders; S5's "no contact shadow" was sampled in the near-black
foreground band 15px below the pools). The scene appears to sit at this
verifier pool's plateau for a dark, dense, many-emitter composition; moving it
likely needs structural work, not fixes.
